Privacy Policy

Rclone Backup is a private application used exclusively by its owner to back up personal files between a private Ubuntu server and Google Drive.

Data Access

The application accesses Google Drive files only as authorized by the account owner for backup, retrieval, and file management.

Data Storage

Backup files are stored on the owner's private server and Google Drive. Sensitive backups are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or intentionally share personal data with third parties. No public user accounts are offered.

Data Retention

Backup files are retained and deleted according to the owner's backup retention settings.

Contact

For questions, contact the application owner through the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
